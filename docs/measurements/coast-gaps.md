# The "no data" strips along coasts, by area (Phase 6 step 2)

Measured by `npm run measure-coast-gaps` for 1 June 1934: the land (Natural Earth 1:10m) that none of the 86 Cliopatria records in effect covers, outside OpenHistoricalMap's East Asia area (where it's the map in these years), sampled every 0.02° (about 2 km).

| Land with no Cliopatria shape | Area |
|---|---|
| Within 25 km of the coast | 1,105,163 km² |
| Further inland | 3,560,007 km² |
| Antarctica (south of 60°S) | 12,058,545 km² |
| For comparison: land the shapes cover | 128,121,628 km² |
